# Knowledge Base for Lalitendra Swamy

This directory contains the knowledge base that will be fed into the RAG chatbot.
The chatbot will use the `.md` files here to answer questions.

## Instructions
1. Replace the contents of the template files (`about_me.md`, `skills.md`, `projects.md`, etc.) with your actual details.
2. The more detail you provide, the better the chatbot will answer.
3. Be descriptive. For projects, mention problem, solution, tech stack, and outcome.
4. After updating these files, run `python ingest.py` to upload them to Pinecone.

## Files to fill out:
- `about_me.md` - Your background, education, and professional summary.
- `skills.md` - Your technical skills and proficiency.
- `projects.md` - Details about your past projects.
- `experience.md` - Your work experience.
- `faqs.md` - Questions you get asked frequently and your preferred answers.
